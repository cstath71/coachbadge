## 1. FootballGPT (SVMS Consultancy Limited, England and Wales; brand 360TFT)

Sources: https://footballgpt.co/affiliates, plus the affiliate agreement shown in a pop-up on https://footballgpt.firstpromoter.com/signup ("I agree to affiliate terms & conditions")

- Affiliates page: "Earn 20% recurring on every Pro subscriber you refer." "Free to join." "You get your link and dashboard once it is approved."
- Agreement 2.1: "a recurring commission on all subscription payments made by customers you directly refer ... for so long as those customers maintain an active subscription ... The standard (Gold) rate is 20 per cent."
- 2.4: commissions are calculated in USD. 2.5: paid on the 1st of each month for the previous month. 2.6: "minimum threshold of $10 USD", with lower amounts rolled over. 2.7: "30-day holding period". 2.8: "(a) PayPal account, or (b) bank transfer."
- 3.2: "within 60 days of clicking your referral link (the 'Cookie Duration')". 3.5: last click.
- Excluded: self-referrals, coupon or cashback placements, and paid ads on FootballGPT or 360TFT brand terms (bidding on these ends the partnership). Links must be disclosed as ads or affiliate links (5.5 gives example wording).
- Governed by the law of England and Wales (10.1).
- Signup form fields: First Name*, Email*, Password*, an optional marketing-email checkbox, the terms checkbox*, and reCAPTCHA. It asks for no website URL, tax, company or phone details. Payout details are needed before any payment (2.9).
- github.io: the form doesn't ask for a website, and nothing on the page or in the agreement mentions website requirements. Approval is manual (1.3, "at its sole discretion").
- Note: the affiliates page now lists admin@360tft.com as the contact. The agreement lists hello@footballgpt.co.
- Separate creator programme (https://footballgpt.co/creators): $20 to $100 per post with 30,000+ views, paid in cash by PayPal or bank transfer, or in Pro months. It doesn't apply to a website.

## 3. CoachTube ("Ambassador" programme; CoachTube, Inc., Texas)

Source: https://coachtube.com/ambassadors

- FAQ: "You get 30% revenue share on all CoachTube courses sold through your ambassador link within a 30-day window."
- FAQ: "We payout ambassador through direct deposit. We require a minimum balance of $100 to process payments to our ambassadors."
- Discrepancy: the page's own tracking script sets the `a=` ambassador cookie with `getExpirationDate(14)`, which looks like 14 days. The FAQ says 30 days. Ask affiliates@coachtube.com if it matters.
- The "Download ambassador terms & agreements" link (https://coachtube.com/coachtube-affiliate-program) returns 404.
- A commented-out line in the page says the programme used to run through ShareASale (US) and Affiliate Window (international). It now runs in-house.

What the ambassador step asks for after login can't be seen without an account. That means payout or tax details weren't verified.
- github.io: not stated anywhere.
- Fit: the site lists "Football (3133)" (American football) and "Soccer (151)". It's a weaker fit for UEFA-badge coaches.

## 5. Amazon Associates UK (alternative, for coaching books)

Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/, the Commission Income Statement at https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ, the Program Policies at https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/operating/policies, and the help page https://affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk/help/node/topic/G7MJTPEP9NC3YKMG

- UK fixed rates: Books / Kindle Books / Audible 5.0%; Sports & Fitness 4.0%; All Other Categories 3.0%.
- Session: ends at the first of "24 hours elapse from that click", an order being placed, or a click on another associate's link. Items added to the cart in the session must be ordered "no later than 89 days after their initial click-through".
- Payment Minimum Chart (UK): Deposit £25, Gift Card £25 (maximum £5,000), Cheque £50.
- Eligibility: "Bloggers, publishers and content creators with a qualifying website or mobile app". After applying, "you have 180 days ... Once you have referred three qualifying sales, we'll evaluate your application".
- Signup needs an Amazon login, so the form wasn't opened. Amazon also collects tax details before paying.
- github.io: Amazon doesn't name github.io anywhere. Approval depends on Amazon's review of the site after three sales.
